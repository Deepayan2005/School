FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Find answers to some common questions from parents.

Which classes are available at the school? +
We offer programmes from early childhood education through primary and secondary school. Please contact the admissions office for the current class availability.
How can I apply for admission? +
Parents can submit an admission enquiry using the form on this website or contact the school office for application details and the admission procedure.
Can parents visit the school campus? +
Yes. Parents can contact the school office to arrange a campus visit and learn more about the facilities.
What extracurricular activities are available? +
Students can participate in activities such as sports, arts, music, cultural events and other programmes offered by the school.
How can I contact the admissions team? +
You can call the school office, send an email or submit the admission enquiry form on this page.
